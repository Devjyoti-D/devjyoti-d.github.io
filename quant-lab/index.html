How it works

  1. Simulate

    Each month, every stock gets six familiar features (momentum, value, quality, low volatility, reversal, size) plus any junk features you add. Next month's return is built from these plus fat-tailed noise, so the true predictability is known and you control it.

  2. Train, then predict

    On each date the model trains only on months that had already finished, then ranks every stock for the coming month. It is retrained on the schedule you choose, so there is no peeking at the future.

  3. Trade

    Buy the stocks it likes most, short the ones it likes least, equal-weighted and dollar-neutral. Costs are charged on everything traded.

  4. Judge

    Sharpe ratio, drawdown and rank IC on unseen months. Compare in-sample skill with out-of-sample skill to spot overfitting.

Read this before drawing conclusions

  • The market is simulated, not real prices. It shows how methods behave when the truth is known, not whether a strategy would make money.
  • Real markets are noisier and their patterns decay, partly because people trade on them. The "Realistic" scenario is still generous.
  • A backtest with a high Sharpe ratio is easy to produce by trying many settings and keeping the best one. Try that here and watch the "Overfitting trap".
  • Nothing on this page is investment advice.

The models (ridge, lasso, gradient-boosted trees and a small neural network) are written from scratch in JavaScript. Source: assets/lab on GitHub.
